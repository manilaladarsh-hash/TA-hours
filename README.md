# TA Hours

A simple teaching assistant hours tracker for keeping your work, course allocations, and remaining hours in one place. Designed for phones, tablets, and desktops, with editable assignments and budgets for your courses.

## Open the tracker

### [Launch TA Hours →](https://manilaladarsh-hash.github.io/TA-hours/)

Open the link and start tracking—no account, download, or technical setup required. This repository contains the website's source code.

## Get started

1. **Choose your assignment.** Select your course, TA role, term, and year. Review the allocated hours against your Hours of Work form.
2. **Log your work.** Enter a duration, use start/end times with breaks, or start a live timer. Press **Stop & save** when finished; stop and restart for breaks.
3. **Review your hours.** See your term total, this week's work, remaining allocation, and time spent in each duty category. Edit entries whenever needed.

Use **Add another course / term** to track additional appointments and switch between them using **Your assignment**. Budgets are planning figures; only recorded work adds to your hours.

## Add it to your home screen

You can use the website directly or add it as an app for easier access.

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open [TA Hours](https://manilaladarsh-hash.github.io/TA-hours/) in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. If shown, enable **Open as Web App**, then tap **Add**.

Open the new **TA Hours** icon to use the tracker. If the option is missing, check **Edit Actions** in the Share menu. [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iphea86e5236/ios)

### Android phones and tablets

1. Open the tracker in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮ menu**, then **Install and create shortcut → Install**. Depending on your version, this may appear as **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Follow the prompts, then open **TA Hours** from your home screen or app list. [Chrome's guide](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/9658361?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en)

On a computer, use the same website link or your browser's app installation option, where available. Load the tracker online once before using it offline.

## Courses and terms

Create an assignment for each course and term, then choose a template or enter your own name and budgets.

Term dates are automatic: **Fall: September 1–December 31**, **Winter: January 1–April 30**, and **Summer: May 1–August 31**.

The work log and CSV report show the selected term by default. If you have entries outside that term, an option lets you include them. All work assigned to an appointment counts toward its remaining allocation. Your signed Hours of Work form takes precedence over a template.

## Your records and backups

Records stay in the browser or app on your device. There are no accounts, analytics, or automatic syncing between devices. Use the same browser or home-screen app consistently, and avoid entering student personal information.

Use **Export backup** regularly to save a complete JSON copy. **Import backup** restores that copy or transfers it to another device; it **replaces** that device's records rather than merging them. Export any existing records first. Clearing browser data or losing a device can remove your records.

For reports, choose **CSV** or **Print**; your browser may also offer Save as PDF.
